# Extreme Heat Days: Guidance

## What is an extreme heat day?

An extreme heat day is defined here as a day in which the maximum daily temperature exceeds the selected temperature threshold, whether it’s an absolute threshold (e.g., 95°F) or a percentile-based threshold. The number of extreme heat days per year is calculated for historical and future global warming levels (GWL): 0.8, 1.5, 2.5, and 3.0°C.
